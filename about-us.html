ABOUT US

Hello, We Are Trifusion Tech Girls

Empowering women through technology and innovation. We are a collective of passionate creators, developers, and designers dedicated to bridging the gender gap in tech and fostering digital inclusion for all. Through collaboration, mentorship, and creativity, we aim to inspire the next generation of women leaders in technology.


Our Story

Trifusion Tech Girls was founded with a shared belief that women belong in every corner of the tech world, from development to leadership. Our journey began as a small group of learners and has grown into a thriving network of women who innovate, create, and inspire. Together, we strive to redefine what it means to be a woman in technology.

Our Identity

We are creators, problem-solvers, and community builders. We embody collaboration, creativity, and courage in everything we do.

Our Vision

To empower women across Africa and the world to thrive in technology-driven industries through education, mentorship, and opportunity.

Our Mission

To inspire and equip young women to pursue careers in technology by providing access to learning resources, networks, and real-world experience.

Our Values

  • Empowerment
  • Innovation
  • Inclusivity
  • Collaboration
